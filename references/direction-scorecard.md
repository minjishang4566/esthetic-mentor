# Direction and Scorecard Guide

Use this guide when the user needs to choose among directions. The output should still feel like a conversation, not a workshop deck.

## Direction Card

```markdown
### [Working name] — [familiar anchor, optional]
**Premise:** [one sentence, including the central tension]
**Visual DNA:** [palette role statement]; [shape/component character]; [type/icon voice].
**Why it fits:** [product and audience reason].
**Watch for:** [one likely failure mode].
```

Avoid a style label that merely repeats the premise. “Soft Bauhaus” is useful only if it tells the user something concrete about geometry, hierarchy, or color behavior.

## Comparison Scorecard

Score each criterion from 1 to 5 based on the product and supplied evidence:

| Criterion | 1 means | 5 means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brand Fit | Could belong to any app or conflicts with brand promise | Distinctly reinforces why this product exists |
| Color Fit | Palette works in isolation but harms clarity or audience fit | Roles support hierarchy, mood, and accessibility intent |
| Shape Fit | Form language conflicts with content and interactions | Forms reinforce the app’s behavior and emotional temperature |
| UI Fit | Attractive reference but fragile in real screens | Scales to navigation, inputs, lists, states, and dense content |
| Personality Fit | Generic or emotionally mismatched | Memorable, appropriate, and recognizably coherent |

Use totals only as a prompt for judgment, never as mathematical proof. A direction with a slightly lower total can be recommended when it better protects the product’s central personality.

## Example: A Pet Memory Journal App

| Direction | Brand | Color | Shape | UI | Personality | Verdict |
| --- | ---:| ---:| ---:| ---:| ---:| --- |
| Gentle Archive | 5 | 5 | 4 | 5 | 5 | Recommended: warm, enduring, and practical |
| Soft Bauhaus Logbook | 4 | 4 | 5 | 5 | 4 | Better if the product wants more energy and structure |
| Candy Sticker Diary | 3 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 5 | Charming, but may make grief or long-term memory feel too light |

The example shows trade-offs rather than a reusable answer. Adapt the three directions to the user’s product.
